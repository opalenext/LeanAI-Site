Privacy

Future of Work · 4 Shifts is a personal project by Vincent Dupuis. It is not linked to any employer.

Your diagnostic answers

Your answers, your organization name and your priorities are saved only in your own browser (local storage), so you can come back to your report. They are not sent to a server and we do not store them. You can delete them at any time by clearing your browser data for this site.

The News Fix front page

When you print the front page, your organization name, your priority challenges, your diagnostic answers on these challenges and the related roadmap initiatives are sent to a server function, which sends them to the Anthropic API (Claude) to write the articles and headlines. We do not store this content.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it. A temporary count of requests per IP address limits the number of front pages per hour. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

No account, no tracking

There is no account, no cookie and no analytics tracker. The site only counts, per month, how many times each step is reached (report, roadmap, front page, image). This count contains no IP address and no identifier.
